Evidence Before Everything

A reader who cannot tell our facts from our analysis is a reader we have failed. This page sets out, as precisely as we can, how The African Governance Stethoscope separates what we know from what we think. The distinction is not a stylistic preference. It is the entire basis on which independent analysis can ask for a reader's trust.

Four labels, applied everywhere

Every finding we publish carries a provenance, and we ask readers to hold us to the sorting.

Documented fact: verifiable against a public record, a contract, a court judgment, an audited account, a gazette. Facts carry citations, and where law and source safety permit, links or document references.

Allegation: a claim by a credible party that we have not confirmed independently. Allegations are attributed, never asserted. If we write that a contract is reportedly structured a certain way, the attribution does the work, and we name who is reporting it.

Analysis: our inference from documented facts. Analysis is argument, and argument can be contested. We aim to address the strongest version of the opposing case, not the one we can easily beat.

Open question: what we do not know. Naming the gap is not weakness. In this line of work the honest identification of an evidentiary void often is the finding. The unregistered creditor, the undisclosed beneficial owner and the unaudited account are all, in themselves, diagnoses.

Our verification standard

Our editorial policy is triangulation across at least three independent source origins for material findings, not three outlets repeating one another. Where a source is an institution reporting on itself, that relationship must remain visible. Linked source records make the evidence inspectable; the presence of three links alone is not proof that every claim has been independently confirmed.

Our correction standard is to publish within 48 hours of a confirmed error, in a public changelog, preserving the original published wording and making the change visible. Pre-publication edits to a submitted draft are not described as corrections to an article that had already appeared.

What we are not

We have no affiliation with any government, party or commercial interest. Our funding policy is that no funder should exceed thirty percent of the budget and no funder should see a publication before our readers do. Funder disclosures and audited annual accounts are a public-disclosure commitment. Those records have not been supplied for this website; we do not claim they are already published.

We are not neutral between evidence and its absence. Where the documented record supports a conclusion, we draw it, carefully and in print, and we stay open to being shown otherwise.

Disclosure status

No audited annual accounts or funder-register files were supplied for this website. The promise to publish them is a commitment, not a link to records we do not have.
